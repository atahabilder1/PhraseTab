PhraseTab turns every new tab into a quick English lesson.

Each time you open a tab, you see a different English phrase, laid out like a dictionary entry:

• The phrase, its type (idiom, phrasal verb, expression or collocation) and its level
• A clear, simple definition
• A natural example sentence
• Pronunciation at the click of a button
• Optional Bangla and Chinese (Simplified) translations

There are 220 hand-written phrases, from everyday ones like "piece of cake" and "call off" to professional ones like "bring someone up to speed" and "take it with a grain of salt".

LEARN AT YOUR OWN PACE
• Mark a phrase as Learning to see it more often, or as Known to see it rarely
• Save phrases to a personal list you can search later
• Choose Beginner, Intermediate or Advanced levels, or all three
• Prefer repetition? Set each phrase to stay for a few tabs before it changes
• Keyboard shortcuts: N next, L learning, K known, S save, P pronounce
• Light and dark themes in a clean black-and-white design

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking
• Works completely offline. Nothing you do ever leaves your computer
• Uses a single permission, storage, to remember your settings and progress on your own device
