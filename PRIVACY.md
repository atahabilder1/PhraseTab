# PhraseTab Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 12 October 2026_

PhraseTab is a Chrome extension that replaces the New Tab page with an English
phrase to learn. It is built to collect nothing.

## What PhraseTab collects

Nothing. PhraseTab does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or
non-personal data.

- No account or sign-in.
- No analytics, telemetry, tracking or advertising.
- No network requests. All phrases, fonts and icons are bundled inside the
  extension, and it works with the network disconnected.
- No access to your browsing history, and no access to the content of any
  website you visit.

## What PhraseTab stores on your device

PhraseTab uses Chrome's `storage` permission to save, **on your own computer
only**:

- your settings (difficulty levels, translation languages, theme, rotation), and
- your learning progress (which phrases you marked Known, Learning or Saved).

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling PhraseTab deletes it. You can
also clear your progress at any time from PhraseTab's Settings page.

## Permissions

PhraseTab requests one permission, `storage`, for the purpose described above.
It requests no host permissions and runs no scripts on other websites.
